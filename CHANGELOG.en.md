# Release notes

English side of the release notes. The French original lives in `CHANGELOG.md`
and drives the announcements: **both files must carry the same version
numbers**, since the bot pairs them to post a bilingual message.

If an entry is missing here, only the French text is published.

Format expected by the bot:

```
## 1.2.0 — Title of the update
An optional opening line.

- A change, written for a player rather than a developer
- Another one
```

---

## 1.3.1 — Readable in both languages

- Positions finally read in English: **GK, CB, CAM, ST** instead of French
  abbreviations, meaningless to anyone who does not speak it.
- In the manager rankings, **connected players move to the top** of the list,
  without their standing being altered.
- Scorers are told apart on sight: yours on the left, the opposition's on the
  right, during the match as well as in the summary.

## 1.3.0 — The derby and the treatment room

Two additions that give a season some edge, and the means to prepare your
matches instead of discovering them.

- A **rival club** is drawn in your division. Matches against them count double
  for morale and pay a bigger bonus when you win.
- A **treatment room** lets you pay to cut a player's injury short. The longer
  the absence, the steeper the bill, and he comes back short of full fitness.
- An **opponent report** appears before every matchday: their position, their
  form over five games, the rating of their squad and the players to watch.
- The live feed now follows the match on its own, and tells your scorers from
  the opposition's.
- Fixed: away from home, the score shown during the match did not match the
  final one.

## 1.2.0 — Welcome to newcomers

This update is for those discovering the game.

- A **Build my squad** button signs a complete, balanced squad in one go: two
  keepers, six defenders, six midfielders, four forwards.
- A **first steps guide** says what to do at each stage, up to your first
  matchday.
- A **manager ranking** shows who is playing, their division, their honours and
  their career season by season.

## 1.1.0 — The January window

- A **January transfer window** opens at mid-season, after the thirteenth
  matchday. The market is narrower and the prices higher: you patch a squad up,
  you do not rebuild it.

## 1.0.0 — YourTeamHistory

- The game is now available in **French and English**, events and dialogues
  included.
- New logo and new name.
- Leagues finally follow a **proper fixture list**: every club meets every other
  twice, home and away.
